# 01_about

Not a chatbot. A folder.

Open the folder in Claude Code. You have a senior business strategist on your screen. It knows your business because you told it once. It knows the $100M methodology cold because it queries a vector index over the full corpus at the moment you ask.

  • Knows your business: written once in /onboard
  • Knows the $100M methodology cold: vector-indexed locally
  • Audits your business across 15 dimensions on demand
  • Writes a 30-day action plan from the audit
  • Checks in on the plan week by week
  • Answers any business question with a real playbook citation

Your data lives in profile.yaml on your disk. Audits and plans are dated markdown files. The Hormozi brain is a 13 MB index file. Nothing leaves your machine through this system.

▼

▼

▼

# 02_install

Install in 5 steps

~5 minutes if you have Claude Code. Add 5 more if you do not.

Install Claude Code

If you do not have it, get it from docs.claude.com/claude-code. Mac, Windows, or Linux all work.

Get the folder

You should have it from whoever sent you the link. The folder is called hormozi-3-books-plus-transcripts. Put it anywhere on your machine.

Install Python dependencies

pip3 install fastembed pymupdf numpy

Total install is ~100 MB. The biggest piece is the embedding model, which downloads once on first query and caches in ~/.cache/huggingface/.

Open the folder in Claude Code

cd /path/to/hormozi-3-books-plus-transcripts
claude

Type /onboard and answer the questions

Eight stages. ~15 minutes. Covers identity, offer, customer, pricing, reach, sales process, operations, and goals. Writes profile.yaml and profile.md at the end.

# 05_internals

How retrieval actually works

Plain English summary below. Want the visual 5-minute walkthrough? →

Think of every paragraph in the $100M corpus as a tiny note card. You have about 8,900 of them sitting in your folder.

Each card has a numerical fingerprint: a list of 384 numbers that capture what the card is about, not the literal words but the meaning. Two cards about pricing have similar fingerprints. A card about pricing and a card about retention have very different fingerprints.

When you ask a question, the strategist converts your question into the same kind of fingerprint. Then it does one simple distance calculation: which 5 cards have fingerprints closest to your question's fingerprint?

Those 5 cards get pulled out and handed to the strategist as context. It reads them, synthesizes an answer, and cites the source playbook by name. That is the whole trick.

The fingerprint generator is a small open-source model that lives on your machine (~80 MB). The card fingerprints sit in a 13 MB file. The distance calculation is one matrix multiplication. The whole thing runs in 200 milliseconds with no internet.

# 06_faq

Frequently asked

The questions I get asked.

Does my data leave my machine?

No. Your profile.yaml, audits, plans, and conversation logs all stay on your disk. The strategist reasons locally. The brain is a local file. The embedding model runs on your CPU.

The only thing that touches the network is Claude Code itself, which routes prompts and responses through Anthropic's servers (because Claude is hosted). Same as any Claude Code session you already use.

What does it cost?

The folder is free. Dependencies are free. The source corpus is publicly available. The only ongoing cost is your Claude Code usage, which you already pay for.

What if I do not have Python?

Most Macs and Linux machines have Python 3 already. Run python3 --version in your terminal. If you get version 3.9 or higher, you are good. If not, install from python.org.

Will it work without internet?

Retrieval works offline once the model is cached and the index is built. The strategist itself (Claude) needs internet because Claude is hosted by Anthropic. So: brain queries are local, the conversation goes through Anthropic.

How do I add my own content to the brain?

Drop PDFs or markdown files into a directory, then run:

python3 brain/ingest.py --source /path/to/your/dir

The script accepts markdown (preferred), PDF, and YouTube-transcript text files. It chunks, embeds, and rebuilds the index. Re-run anytime to add or refresh material.

Can I share this with my business partner?

Yes, with limits. The LICENSE in the folder says personal-use, not redistribution. If your partner works on the same business with you, you can both use the same folder. For a different friend with a different business, get them their own copy and let them onboard fresh.

What if the strategist is wrong?

Tell it. Truth hierarchy: what you just said > profile.yaml > audit findings > $100M frameworks. If a framework says one thing and your numbers say another, your numbers win and the strategist surfaces the conflict.

How fresh is the corpus?

The shipped index covers the three core books and source video content up to early 2026.

Why these three books and not more?

The three core $100M books cover the load-bearing methodology: how to construct an offer, how to get leads, and how to design a money model. The supplementary playbooks distill from these. By indexing the books in their original form plus the full video archive, you get the source material as the author wrote and said it, not in condensed form. Synthesis still crosses topics: a pricing question can pull from $100M Offers, $100M Money Models, and a video that ties them together, in one retrieval.
